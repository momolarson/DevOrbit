## Problem
Writing a self-review means trawling three months of GitHub history for
what you actually shipped. Everyone dreads it and everyone under-reports.

## Proposal
The same facts-then-narrative pipeline as the weekly retro, over a 90 day
window, with a different prompt: group by theme, lead with impact,
link every claim to a PR. Export as markdown.

## Notes
Low effort once the retro exists. High value a few times a year. Good
demo material because everyone recognises the pain.
