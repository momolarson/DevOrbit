## Problem
We do not know if an engineer has a weekly reason to open this. Everything
else depends on the answer.

## Proposal
For two weeks, keep a plain text note. At the end of each week write down
what you wish a tool had told you about your own work: a PR that sat too
long, a review you rushed, a pattern you only noticed in hindsight. No
building during this period.

At the end, read the note. If it has real entries, they become the first
retro templates. If it is empty, archive the project.

## Notes
This is the cheapest possible test of the product hypothesis. Skipping it is
how the first version ended up with six dashboards and no user.
